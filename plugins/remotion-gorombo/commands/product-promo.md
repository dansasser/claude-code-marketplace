# Product Promo Video

Create a promotional video for a product or service by researching its URL. 5 scenes with hook, solution, capabilities, trust, and CTA.

**Usage:** `/product-promo <URL>`

**Examples:**
- `/product-promo https://gorombo.com/managed-ai`
- `/product-promo https://example.com/product`

Invokes the `remotion-gorombo:product-promo` skill with the provided arguments.
